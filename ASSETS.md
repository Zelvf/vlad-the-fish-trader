# Visual assets

`public/vlad-placeholder.png` is a generated **temporary** aquarium visual. It is not a photograph of Vlad and is never used as proof that the camera is online. The website says “Temporary visual” beside it and shows tracker/broadcast states separately.

Built-in image generation was used with this final prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: temporary website hero and offline camera still for a simulated fish trading dashboard. Primary request: a photorealistic cinematic image of a single small orange-and-white goldfish in a real glass aquarium, front-facing three-quarter profile, swimming near the middle-right of the frame. Scene/backdrop: deep ink-blue water with restrained teal caustic reflections, subtle air bubbles and soft aquatic plants, believable home aquarium. Composition: wide 16:9 landscape, fish in right half with generous clear water on left for interface overlay; natural tank depth, sharp fish and gently blurred background. Lighting/mood: elegant documentary aquarium photography, nuanced highlights, sophisticated and calm with a hint of playful character. Constraints: one fish only, realistic anatomy, no people, no text, no logo, no watermark, no stock chart, no interface.

The fish mark in the header and `public/favicon.svg` are original code-native SVG assets. The website uses Lucide interface icons. The Michael Reeves reference video supplied by the user is linked for context; no frames or audio from it are packaged here.

When Vlad photos or clips are available, replace the still with permission from their owner, update the alt text, and recalibrate the tracker for Vlad's true color and camera angle.
